What an AI Visibility Audit Actually Checks

Before we recommend anything, we measure where you stand. Here's exactly what our $49 audit covers — so you know what you're getting.

Most "AI visibility" reports are a screenshot of one ChatGPT answer. That tells you almost nothing. A real audit measures your presence across engines, queries, and competitors — then turns it into a roadmap. Here's what we actually check.

1. Your core queries

We start with the searches that matter to your revenue: your main service category, comparison queries ("X vs Y"), and question-based queries ("how much does X cost?", "best X for [audience]"). These are the queries where AI recommendations become customers.

2. Five engines, not one

Visibility is not just ChatGPT. We check your presence across:

  • ChatGPT (with and without search)
  • Google AI Overviews
  • Perplexity
  • Claude
  • Gemini

Each engine sources differently, so a brand cited by one may be absent from others. You need to know where you stand in each.

3. You vs. competitors

For every query, we record who gets named — you, your direct competitors, or neither. This is the single most useful output: it shows exactly who's beating you and in which engines. If a competitor appears in 6 of 8 answers and you appear in 1, the roadmap starts with the gap.

4. Source analysis

When an AI answer names a competitor, we trace which source it pulled from. That reveals the authority pages in your niche — directories, publications, review platforms — that you need to be on. This is the GEO half of the fix.

5. Distribution gaps

We flag the specific articles and pages that cite competitors but not you. These are the fastest wins: content already positioned to be read by AI engines, missing only your brand.

6. Entity and content health

We check the basics that cap your visibility: name/category/location consistency, structured data on your site, and whether your pages answer your core queries (AEO readiness).

What you get

  • A visibility scorecard per engine and per query
  • Competitor gap analysis
  • Distribution gaps — where to get placed
  • A 90-day roadmap, prioritized by impact

That roadmap is the whole point. Not a screenshot — a plan you can execute, with the specific gaps and placements that will move your AI visibility first.
